## Description

Ready to make your mark on the open source world? Whether you are a veteran contributor or looking to submit your very first pull request, this hands-on session is your chance to work directly alongside core Node.js maintainers and contribute directly to Node.js core. We will kick things off with a brief presentation covering open source ethos, official contributing guidelines (including appropriate AI usage), and tips for how to effectively engage with the project and source contribution ideas. Then, we’ll dive straight into live coding with a curated list of ready-to-go Node.js contributions. All attendees of all skill levels will have something to work on.

At JSConf México 2026 in Guadalajara, the workshop runs on October 29 from 15:00 to 17:30.
